# 🌴 Frame In Goa - Hacker House Goa 2026

**Frame In Goa** is a custom graphic generator built for attendees of **Hacker House Goa 2026** (Oct 28-31, 2026). It allows the 247 participating builders to easily create, customize, and download personalized artwork to share their excitement and represent the event. 

Check out the live deployment here: [hhgoa-one.vercel.app](https://hhgoa-one.vercel.app)

## ✨ Features

*   **📸 Drag & Drop Uploads:** Seamlessly upload your photo by clicking or dragging it into the dropzone. Supports JPG, PNG, and HEIC formats, regardless of crop or orientation.
*   **🎨 Multiple Custom Templates:** Switch between different custom-designed graphic themes:
    *   `01` Face Flag
    *   `02` Builder Pass
    *   `03` Signal Banner
    *   `04` Crew Collage
*   **👀 Real-time Preview:** See your custom artwork generate in real-time within the app interface.
*   **🚀 Goa Dispatch:** Quickly finalize and download your customized graphic to share on social media.
